## intro
> title: From spreadsheet to target state | for architects

You already know your landscape. It's just spread over spreadsheets, wikis and diagrams that
went stale the day they were drawn. Here is how Atrium turns that into one model you can trust,
in an afternoon.

## import
> shot: import

Start with what you have. Drop in an export from your CMDB, an Excel list or an ArchiMate file.
Atrium works out what each column means, matches rows to what is already there so nothing is
duplicated, and shows exactly what will change before you click Import. Next time, it remembers
the layout.

## autofill
> shot: autofill

Fields that can be worked out are filled in for you: versions and end-of-life dates for
technology, owners and domains inherited from their parents, and likely links between
applications and capabilities. Suggestions stay grey until you accept them, one at a time or
all at once.

## map
> shot: explore-map

Now the picture. The capability map shows which applications support what, coloured by health,
cost, lifecycle or fit. Click any box and the inspector opens beside it, so you can edit in place
without losing your view.

## graph
> shot: graph

The dependency graph starts with the big picture: your domains and how much they talk to each
other. Drill into one, or centre on a single application and ask what breaks if it goes.

## target
> shot: scenario

Then plan the future. Switch to the target state and simply edit: retire a system, add a new
platform, move a capability. Every change is tracked as a difference from today. The time slider
and the roadmap show when it all happens.

## review
> shot: review

Nothing changes behind your back. Edits from colleagues outside their area, and from coding
agents, arrive as suggestions with a clear before and after. Approve in two clicks, and undo if
you change your mind.

## reference
> shot: packs

Industry reference models plug in as a separate layer. Install a pack, and Atrium suggests how
your capabilities map to it. Accept the good matches in one go and see where you differ from the
standard. The telecom pack for TM Forum's frameworks uses the same mechanism.

## close
> end: Your model, current and trusted, without the busywork.

Import what you have, let Atrium fill the gaps, plan the target, and keep it honest with
reviews. Your model, current and trusted, without the busywork.
