## intro
> title: Decisions in three clicks | for managers with a technical background

You need straight answers. What do we run, what does it cost, where is the risk, and are we
moving in the right direction? Atrium puts those answers one click from the landing page.

## health
> shot: dashboard

The portfolio dashboard shows how healthy your applications are, where the money goes per domain,
and which systems to invest in, tolerate, migrate or eliminate. Health is worked out from fit
ratings and technology lifecycles, so it stays current without anyone updating a slide.

## drill
> shot: drill

Every number is a door. Click the share that needs action and you see the applications behind
it, with the reasons: unsupported technology, poor fit, or a platform being phased out.

## cost
> shot: cost

Cost per capability shows what the business actually pays for each thing it does, not just per
system. It is the fastest way to spot duplication: three applications doing the same job.

## risk
> shot: eol

End-of-life exposure lists the technologies that are out of support, or soon will be, and which
applications rely on them. That is your risk register, built from facts.

## roadmap
> shot: roadmap

The roadmap shows initiatives and system lifecycles on one timeline. Ask a question in plain
words, such as what breaks if we retire the legacy CRM, and get the answer straight away.

## present
> shot: present

When it is time to present, click Share and choose PowerPoint. You get real, editable charts in
your own deck, not screenshots. The same data is always one click away as Excel.

## close
> end: Answers you can trust, in the time it takes to ask.
